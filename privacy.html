Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

1. Overview

Frost is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data when you use the Frost Minecraft Client, our website, or our other services (the launcher, our Discord server, the store, and the creator dashboard). We've written this to reflect what we actually collect today, not a generic template — if something below changes, this page changes with it.

2. Information We Collect

Your Microsoft sign-in. Signing in to your Minecraft account happens directly inside a Microsoft-hosted window — we never see your password. The resulting Microsoft/Xbox/Minecraft tokens are stored only on your own device, never on our servers. See our Security page for the full detail, including the one fallback case where your Minecraft token briefly passes through our server on its way to Mojang's own API for verification (never stored, never logged).

Discord sign-in. Reviews, staff/creator applications, the partner dashboard, statistics pages and our admin tools sign you in with Discord. This shares your Discord user ID, username and avatar with us, and — only for staff/creator tools — your server role membership. We never see your Discord password.

Minecraft account & gameplay data. Once your Discord and Minecraft accounts are linked, we store your Minecraft username and UUID against your Discord ID, so we can verify ownership of cosmetics (cloaks) and Frost Client Lite. While you're playing, the launcher/mod reports a lightweight "still online" signal — your Minecraft username and launcher/mod version. We use it for the live player count and for your public player profile: from it we keep the date we first saw your username, the time we last saw it, and one running total of your FrostClient playtime. We don't keep a history of individual sessions, and we don't collect which server you're playing on. Our statistics pages don't publish a list of who is online. We never see anything about singleplayer worlds, and the launcher doesn't collect your IP address except for ban enforcement, described below.

Public player profiles. Every FrostClient player has a public profile on player.frostclient.eu that anyone can find by Minecraft username. It shows your Minecraft username and skin, your equipped Frost cloak, the cloaks you own, whether you have Frost Client Lite, when you joined FrostClient, and — unless you turn them off — your total playtime and whether you're playing right now. Your linked Discord account (name and avatar) is shown too, and people can find your profile by your Discord ID, unless you turn off Show linked Discord account. Members of the FrostClient staff team have a staff badge on their profile. You control all of this under Account → Privacy settings (the gear icon) on our website. Frost Client Lite subscribers can also make their profile private: anyone who looks it up then only sees that the profile is private. If you haven't linked a Discord account yet, your profile uses the default settings — sign in to the launcher with Discord to change them, or ask us on Discord to hide it.

Ban enforcement. To enforce bans, the launcher sends us a one-way hashed identifier of your device. It's derived from your computer's hardware ID, and the original ID can't be recovered from it. When you sign in, our server stores this identifier together with your Discord ID, your Minecraft account and the IP address it saw at sign-in. We use this only to enforce bans, so that a banned player can't come back on a new Discord account. If you believe a ban is a mistake, you can appeal it here.

Cosmetics & rewards. Your selected Frost cloak and your Discord server boost count (used to grant Frost Client Lite) are stored against your Discord ID.

Bug reports & support tickets. If you submit a bug report or open a support ticket, we store what you send us — including message content and any attachments — to help staff resolve it. Ticket conversations are kept in a staff-only archive for reference (oldest entries are dropped once the archive fills up) and, if you're given a transcript link, that public link expires and is deleted automatically after 24 hours.

Reviews & applications. If you post a public review or apply for a staff/creator role, we store the content you submit (rating, comment, application answers) along with your Discord identity.

Payments. If you purchase Frost Client Lite, a cloak, or receive a creator payout, our payment provider (Whop) and our payout process share your name and email (for purchases) or PayPal email (for creator withdrawals) with us, so we can confirm and process the transaction.

3. What We Don't Do

We don't set tracking cookies — the website uses your browser's local storage only to remember things like your sign-in session and preferences on your own device. We don't run third-party analytics or advertising/tracking scripts. We don't sell or share your data with advertisers.

4. Third-Party Services

Frost integrates with the following third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Microsoft / Xbox / Mojang — Minecraft account sign-in and profile verification
  • Discord — sign-in, community features, and support tickets
  • Modrinth — mod distribution
  • Whop — payment processing for Frost Client Lite and cosmetics
  • Google (Sheets/Apps Script) — internal record-keeping for reviews, creator payouts, and entitlements

5. Data Retention

Public ticket transcript links expire and are deleted after 24 hours. The staff-only ticket archive is capped in size and drops its oldest entries once full. Account-linked data (Discord ID, Minecraft username/UUID, cloak selection, boost/Lite status, profile privacy settings) and profile data (first-seen date, last-seen time, total playtime) are kept for as long as it's relevant to providing that feature, or until you ask us to remove it. You may request deletion of any personally identifiable data by reaching out via our Discord server.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete personal data we hold about you. To exercise these rights, contact us through our Discord server.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

8. Contact

For privacy-related questions, reach out on our Discord server.
